# Design

Transcribed from the live site, `PRODUCT.md`, and the styles already in `app/globals.css` and `tailwind.config.js`. This is the owner's identity. Do not restyle it into another product.

## Identity

Personal homepage for Respire (RimunAce). Friends should be able to find the diary, the photos, the music, and the anime lists. Kasane Teto is the redhead on the page. The old site stays at other.respire.my.

## Personality

A diary with a hard edge. Playful, specific, a little loud. The voice in the bio and the updates is the owner's. Do not sand it into marketing copy.

## Palette

- Paper gray `#E0E0E0` (page)
- Black `#000000` (ink, borders, header bar)
- White `#FFFFFF` (cards)
- Gold `#FFD700` (one warning and status accent, used sparingly)
- Pink field `#FFB6C1` on the canvas behind the cards
- Miku `#39C5BB` only for the Miku playlist switch

Neutrals are the paper, ink, and card. Pink is the room behind the cards. Gold is the single accent.

## Typography

Courier New for body and UI text: it is the diary voice this page already has.
Arial for headings: a plain bold label so section names stay readable against the mono body.
Headings are short uppercase labels with 1px tracking. That tracking is the existing retro label, not a wide display treatment.

## Layout

Cards with a 2px black border and a hard 4px black offset shadow. The shadow is a card sitting on the desk, not a soft float.
Home order is the owner's: about, then updates beside contact, then projects, then the webring.
The canvas grid and the slow fog are the room. Keep them. The grid is this page's graph paper, not a generic tech backdrop.

## Dials

ENERGY 2. The page says hello with the black bar, the pink room, and the Teto figure, then settles into the diary.
RHYTHM 2. Sections share the card system and then break it: the bio is a portrait plus prose, updates are a dated list, contact is a short list with the figure, projects are rows, the webring is a three-part strip.
MOTION 2. Hover and press on the retro buttons, the news ticker, the fog, and the player opening. Nothing choreographed. Reduced motion turns the ticker, the fog, and the canvas follow-along off.

## Motif

Hard black offset shadow, repeated. One pink room behind white cards.
